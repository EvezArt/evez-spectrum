## 5. OFFER ARCHITECTURE

### Offer A - $5k-$10k Technical Diligence Sprint
Positioning: "A decision-grade technical risk and evidence memo in 10 business days."
Use cases: autonomy assurance; PQC exposure; scientific-ML validation; quantum use-case assessment; startup/investment diligence.
Standard method: kickoff and artifact request; two to four expert interviews; architecture and claims map; targeted repository/data review; failure-mode analysis; evidence ledger; executive readout.
Deleliverables: 10-20-page memo, one-page executive decision brief, evidence/assumption register, prioritized 30/60/90-day backlog, and a live readout. No implementation, legal opinion, safety certification, penetration test, or cryptographic validation unless separately scoped.
Pricing logic: $5k for a narrow system or one decision; $7.5k default; $10k when scope includes multiple subsystems, a live demonstration, or a board/investment committee readout. These are EVEZ target prices, not verified market averages.

### Offer B - $15k Briefing / Training
Positioning: "Turn a technically confused leadership group into a shared decision model."
Format: two half-days or a compact workshop series, preceded by document review and followed by a written decision pack.
Modules: scientific-ML validation; evidence for autonomous systems; agent orchestration and control; PQC migration; quantum use-case triage; technical claim writing for customers and investors.
Deliverables: customized briefing deck, exercises using the client's architecture, decision rubric, glossary, post-session action memo. Avoid generic motivational training.

### Offer C - $3k-$5k/month Advisory
Positioning: "Part-time deep-tech systems and evidence advisor for high-consequence technical decisions."
Cadence: one weekly working session, asynchronous document review, one monthly executive memo, capped technical reviews. Define response times and exclusions.

Retainer discipline: Do not promise unlimited access. A retainer should convert the initial sprint into recurring decision support, not become unbounded staff augmentation.

## 6. VALUE QUANTIFICATION

The value case is framed as cost of uncertainty avoided or decision time accelerated, never as an unsupported ROI guarantee.

| Buyer problem | Conservative quantification model | Evidence required before quoting value |
|---|---|---|
| One blocked engineering or customer decision | Value = loaded team cost per week x weeks accelerated | Baseline decision cycle, participants, milestone impact |
| Repeated simulation runs | Value = runs avoided x cost/run + engineering hours released | Solver cost, compute cost, engineer time, accuracy threshold |
| Autonomy test coverage gap | Value = risk-adjusted milestone delay avoided; do not equate test count with safety | Current scenario inventory, failure history, review requirements |
| PQC discovery gap | Value = remediation rework avoided + exposure reduction | Asset inventory, contract deadlines, system criticality |
| Investment diligence | Value = downside avoided or decision time reduced; do not claim investment alpha | Fund's loss case, memo cycle, technical review history |

Illustrative, not a promise: if a five-person technical group with a loaded weekly cost of $20k spends three weeks resolving an architecture question, a $7.5k diligence sprint that reduces the cycle by one week has an arithmetic value of about $20k before considering risk. The input is an assumption and must be replaced with the buyer's numbers. Similarly, if a scientific team runs 200 expensive simulations and a validated surrogate reduces only 25% of those runs, the benefit can be modeled, but only after measuring accuracy and rework.

## 10. CHANNEL MIX AND CONVERSION PATH

Warm introductions first: former colleagues, researchers, founders, accelerator mentors, security architects, V&V leads, VC platform teams. Publish one technical artifact per month: autonomy evidence schema, PQC inventory worksheet, scientific-ML validation checklist, or claim-ledger example. GitHub as proof; LinkedIn/email as distribution. Offer a 20-minute scoping conversation, but sell a paid artifact rather than free consulting.

Conversion path: public artifact -> short diagnostic call -> fixed-scope $5k-$10k diligence -> $15k team briefing or implementation partner handoff -> $3k-$5k/month advisory. Every project creates a sanitized case study, reusable template, or benchmark, subject to client permission.

## 11. 30/60/90-DAY PIPELINE PLAN

### Days 1-30: package and validate
- Choose one primary wedge: autonomy evidence, PQC migration intelligence, or scientific-ML validation. Keep the other two as adjacent offers.
- Publish a one-page EVEZ advisory site with three offers, exclusions, biography, proof-pack index.
- Create three runnable demos: event provenance; agent decision trace; simulation/model validation report. Reproducible setup.
- Produce a two-page sample diligence memo using clearly labeled synthetic or public data.
- Conduct 20 warm conversations and 20 targeted outbound messages. Goal is not volume; it is learning which risk is already funded.
- Target outcome: 5 qualified conversations, 2 scoped opportunities, 1 paid pilot.

### Days 31-60: close and specialize
- Deliver the first pilot with a strong executive memo and readout.
- Ask for one reference, one anonymized case-study permission, one referral.
- Run a $15k briefing pilot with a technical team or investor group.
- Build partner list: safety/V&V consultancy, PQC implementation firm, cloud/GPU integrator, domain scientist.
- Test two price points and one smaller diagnostic offer; record objections and procurement friction.
- Target outcome: 2 paid projects total, 1 retainer proposal, 1 partner referral source.

### Days 61-90: convert to repeatability
- Turn the best pilot into a named product: checklist, standard inputs, standard report, standard readout.
- Publish one sanitized case study: baseline, work performed, decision changed, limitations.
- Approach 10 deep-tech funds or corporate venture teams with the claim-ledger diligence offer.
- Approach 15 autonomy/scientific-ML/PQC accounts with a trigger-based message.
- Convert one client to a three-month advisory retainer. If nobody converts, narrow the offer before adding features.
- Target outcome: 1-2 retainers, 3-5 completed paid sprints, pipeline worth at least 3x next-quarter revenue target.
